# Data

The original research data are not distributed in this public portfolio repository.

The analytical workflow expects tabular NDVI observations containing, at minimum:

- `date`: observation date
- `NDVI`: mean NDVI value for the study area
- a study-site identifier when multiple sites are processed together

The research workflow analyses three Mediterranean grassland sites in the Community of Madrid, Spain: Piñuecar, Colmenar Viejo, and Tielmes.

Raw satellite products, processed raster data, intermediate files, and research datasets are intentionally excluded from this repository.
