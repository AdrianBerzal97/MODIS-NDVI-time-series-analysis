Individual per-site NDVI time series were also supplied, but the combined plot already communicates their principal portfolio purpose. They are omitted to reduce redundancy.

## Interpretation

Cumulative NDVI visualizations illustrate within-year aggregation and shared temporal structure. They do not by themselves validate a predictive relationship with SIMPAST biomass. The [supplementary first-difference results](../results/supplementary_table_S1_first_differences.csv) offer an important complementary sensitivity check.
